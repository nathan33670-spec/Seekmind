SeekMind privacy policy

Effective [TO FILL IN: date]. Version 2.

The essentials

  1. Everything stays on your phone. What SeekMind remembers is encrypted on the device and never leaves it: not to the publisher, not to the ad network, not to anyone.
  2. The publisher receives nothing. No account, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no server.
  3. Nothing is read without your consent. Reading only starts once you have agreed explicitly and chosen which apps to remember. You can pause it or withdraw your consent at any time.
  4. Sensitive content is left out from the start. Passwords, banking, authentication, private browsing and protected screens are never remembered; card numbers and IBANs are masked.
  5. No image is ever stored, and SeekMind never acts on your behalf in other apps.
  6. Ads are shown only when you ask, and they receive nothing SeekMind has remembered.

1. Who publishes the app

SeekMind is published by [TO FILL IN: publisher’s name]. Contact for any question about your data: [TO FILL IN: support e-mail address].

What SeekMind remembers is processed only on your phone, under your sole control: the publisher has no access to it and receives no copy of it. The app’s source code is public, on GitHub, so that anyone can check these commitments.

2. What SeekMind reads, and why

To let you find again what you have seen, SeekMind uses Android’s accessibility service (the AccessibilityService API), after your explicit consent.

  • It reads the text shown in the apps you chose to remember, the app’s name, the screen’s title and the address of web pages.
  • When a screen holds mostly pictures or video, it occasionally analyses the screen image to recognise text in it. That image only exists in memory, for a moment: it is never stored, on the phone or anywhere else.
  • The only purpose is to build your personal search index, on your phone. This data is not used for advertising, audience measurement or profiling.

What SeekMind never reads. Password fields, private browsing, screens protected by the app that shows them, sensitive apps (password managers and authenticators, always; banking, payment, health and public administration, unless you include them yourself) and SeekMind itself. Card numbers, IBANs and French social security numbers are masked before anything is stored. The service is passive: it performs no action, gesture or input in other apps.

4. Legal basis

Reading the screen is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR), given in the app after a screen that describes exactly what is read. It is dated, it covers a given version of that text, and it can be withdrawn at any time in Settings › Memorisation, which stops all reading at once. If the substance of that screen changes, the app asks for your consent again.

For ads, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is collected by Google’s consent form before the first ad (see §6).

5. Security

  • Encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256, integrity checked page by page). Its key is itself encrypted by a hardware key of the phone that the system never lets out (Android Keystore, secure chip where available).
  • App lock by fingerprint, face or the phone’s code, on by default.
  • Android’s automatic backups and transfers are turned off: your data does not go into an online backup.
  • No technical log holds any text read, title, address or search.
  • Nothing is copied to the clipboard without an action of yours.
  • “Erase everything” destroys the database and its key.

6. Ads

The version published on Google Play offers rewarded ads from Google (AdMob), to earn search credits. An ad is loaded when you open the “Keep searching” sheet, which also opens by itself when your credits run out; it is only shown if you tap “Watch an ad”. No other ad is shown: no banner, no forced full-screen ad.

At that point, Google’s SDK collects data, according to your choices: the device’s advertising ID, technical data (model, system, language), the IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred) and interactions with the ad. Google processes this data as a controller, including outside the European Union, as described in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services” and in Google’s privacy policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form appears before the first ad. You can change your choices at any time in Settings › Activation › Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s settings.

What SeekMind has remembered is never sent to the ad network: the ad module is isolated from the rest of the app and receives no user data; no ad request carries a keyword, an address or any targeting drawn from your screens or your searches. Until the “Keep searching” sheet has been opened, the app sends nothing over the network.

A version without ads and without Internet access exists; it never connects to the network.

7. Language packs

Optional language packs are downloaded by Google Play, only when you ask. Google Play handles that download like that of any app; SeekMind adds no data to it.

8. Activation codes

An activation code is checked on your phone, offline. No data is sent to activate it. The web page that relays activation links sends nothing: the code stays in your browser.

10. Your controls and your rights

  • Choose the families of apps remembered (messaging, browsers, social networks, others) and exclude or include an app.
  • Pause memorisation, from the app or the quick settings tile; withdraw your consent.
  • See everything that was remembered, in the journal; delete a capture, a period, an app, or erase everything; set how long captures are kept.
  • Protect the app with your fingerprint or code.
  • Turn the accessibility service off, which stops all reading.

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability set out in the GDPR. Since your data is only on your phone, you exercise them directly in the app: the publisher holds no data about you and can therefore neither see nor disclose it. For advertising data, contact Google. For any question, write to the address in §1. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in France, the CNIL).

11. Deleting your data

SeekMind creates no account and keeps nothing outside your phone: there is nothing to delete anywhere else.

  • One capture: in the journal, tap it, then “Delete”.
  • A period or an app: Settings › Data.
  • Everything: Settings › Data › Erase everything (the database and its key are destroyed).
  • Automatically: beyond the retention period you chose.
  • For good: uninstalling SeekMind deletes all its data.
