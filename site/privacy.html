1. Who publishes the app

2. What SeekMind reads, and why

To let you find again what you saw, SeekMind uses Android’s accessibility service (AccessibilityService API), after your explicit consent.

  • It reads the text shown in the apps you use, the name of the app, the title of the screen and the address of web pages.
  • When a screen holds mostly images or video, it occasionally analyses the image of the screen to recognise text in it. This image is processed in memory and never saved.
  • The only purpose is to build, on your phone, your personal search index.

What SeekMind does not read. Password fields, private browsing, protected screens and sensitive apps (banking, password managers…) are never remembered. Card numbers and IBANs are masked before anything is stored. SeekMind never takes any action for you in other apps.

3. Where the data is

Everything SeekMind remembers (text, titles, addresses, search index) stays on your phone, in an encrypted database whose key is protected by the system. Nothing is sent, neither to the publisher, nor to the ad network, nor to anyone. There is no account. Android’s automatic backups and transfers are turned off for this data.

4. What the publisher receives

Nothing. SeekMind collects no usage statistics and sends no crash reports.

5. Advertising

  • SeekMind offers rewarded ads provided by Google (AdMob), to earn search credits. An ad is loaded when you open the “Keep searching” sheet, which also opens by itself when you run out of credits; it is shown only if you tap “Watch an ad”. No other ad is shown.
  • When it does, Google’s SDK collects data, according to your choices: the device’s advertising ID, technical data (model, system, language), IP address and interactions with the ad. This processing is Google’s, as described in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before the first ad. You can change your choices at any time in Settings › Activation › Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s settings.
  • What SeekMind remembered is never passed to the ad network: no ad request carries a keyword, an address or any targeting taken from your screens or your searches. Until that sheet has been opened, the app sends nothing over the network.

6. Language packs

Optional language packs are downloaded by Google Play, at your request. Google Play handles this download like that of any app; SeekMind adds no data to it.

7. Your controls

  • Choose which families of apps are remembered (messaging, browsers, social media, others) and exclude apps.
  • Pause remembering, from the app or from the quick settings tile.
  • Delete a capture, a period, an app or everything; set how long captures are kept (90 days by default).
  • Protect the app with your fingerprint or your PIN.
  • Turn off the accessibility service, which stops all reading. Uninstalling the app deletes all its data.

8. Activation codes

An activation code is checked on your phone, offline. No data is sent to activate it. The web page that relays activation links sends nothing: the code stays in your browser.
